# Product Context: TechAdvisor AI

## 1. Identity & Mission
TechAdvisor AI is an authoritative hardware intelligence, PC configurator, and multi-retailer price comparison platform for Australia, India, and global markets. It combines the rigorous benchmark credibility of RTINGS, the compatibility engine of PCPartPicker, the lab depth of Tom's Hardware, and the trusted buying guidance of Wirecutter.

## 2. Primary Users & Jobs To Be Done
- **Hardware Enthusiasts & Builders**: Validating socket, DDR5/DDR4, wattage headroom, and GPU case clearances without friction or clutter.
- **Value-Conscious Shoppers**: Finding the lowest real price across verified retailers (Amazon, Scorptec, MDComputers, PrimeABGB, Centre Com) and setting target price drop alerts.
- **Mainstream Buyers**: Seeking fast, unambiguous advice (*"What is the best laptop for Adobe Photoshop and Office?"*) without technical jargon or deceptive affiliate spam.

## 3. Surface Modes (Impeccable Hierarchy)
- **`Operate` (The Builder & Comparison Engines)**:
  - High information density, scannable tabular specifications, monospace metrics (`AM5`, `DDR5-6000`, `320W`, `₹58,999`), zero decorative fluff, instant feedback.
- **`Persuade` / `Read` (Landing, Guides & Product Detail)**:
  - Editorial authority, high-contrast typography, real product photography, clean badge hierarchy, transparent disclosures.

## 4. Anti-Patterns To Reject ("AI Slop" Directives)
- No purple-to-blue neon gradients.
- No blurry neon glow box-shadows.
- No rounded-square icon tiles floating above every single heading.
- No 3-to-4 layer nested card-in-card containers.
- No low-contrast gray text on dark gray cards.

## 5. Strategic Roadmap & Expansion
- **Phase 1 (Current)**: Hardware Dominance (PC Builder, GPUs, CPUs, Laptops, Storage, Monitors, Regional Pricing).
- **Phase 2 (Growth Milestone)**: Hybrid Hardware + AI Tool Subscriptions & SaaS Marketplace. Complete blueprint documented in [`AI_TOOLS_ROADMAP.md`](./AI_TOOLS_ROADMAP.md).
